# Commands

The `wiki-sticky` CLI surface: scaffolding, the mechanical lint gate, and the
dependency-editing commands that drive the composition layer.

* [wiki-sticky init](/wiki/commands/init.md) - scaffolds the bundle, config, skills and pre-commit hook
* [wiki-sticky lint](/wiki/commands/lint.md) - the 12 mechanical checks, and which ones only warn
* [wiki-sticky install](/wiki/commands/install.md) - resolve, vendor and lock; what --frozen verifies
* [add, rm and update](/wiki/commands/add-rm-update.md) - the three commands that edit wiki-sticky.yaml and re-sync
