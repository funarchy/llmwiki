# Bundle format

The OKF-conformant page format llmwiki holds bundles to: what a page looks
like, what an index file does, how links must be written, and where
about-the-bundle pages belong.

* [Page anatomy](/llmwiki/format/page-anatomy.md) - required frontmatter, the one relative path, and why there's no `updated:` field
* [Index files](/llmwiki/format/index-files.md) - why index.md carries no frontmatter and how reachability is checked
* [Links](/llmwiki/format/links.md) - why body links are reference-style and repo-root-absolute
* [Meta pages and _meta/](/llmwiki/format/meta-pages.md) - type: meta concept pages versus the tooling-only _meta directory
