# Commands

The `llmwiki` CLI surface: scaffolding, the mechanical lint gate, and the
dependency-editing commands that drive the composition layer.

* [llmwiki init](/llmwiki/commands/init.md) - scaffolds the bundle, config, skills and pre-commit hook
* [llmwiki lint](/llmwiki/commands/lint.md) - the 12 mechanical checks, and which ones only warn
* [llmwiki install](/llmwiki/commands/install.md) - resolve, vendor and lock; what --frozen verifies
* [add, rm and update](/llmwiki/commands/add-rm-update.md) - the three commands that edit llmwiki.yaml and re-sync
